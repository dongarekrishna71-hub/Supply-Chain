# 📦 Supply Chain Performance Dashboard – Power BI Project

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Supply Chain Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution designed to monitor, analyze, and optimize supply chain operations. The dashboard provides real-time insights into order fulfillment, delivery performance, warehouse efficiency, supplier performance, inventory management, and logistics costs.

This project enables businesses to make data-driven decisions by transforming raw supply chain data into meaningful visualizations and actionable insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Monitor overall supply chain performance.
* Track order and delivery status.
* Analyze warehouse and supplier efficiency.
* Evaluate logistics and shipping costs.
* Monitor inventory levels and reorder points.
* Identify operational bottlenecks and improvement opportunities.
* Support strategic decision-making through data visualization.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### Delivery Performance

* 74.4% of orders experienced delivery delays.
* Average delivery time is approximately 8 days.
* Most deliveries are completed within 5–10 days.

### Warehouse Performance

* WH-Ahmedabad demonstrates the best delivery efficiency.
* WH-Mumbai requires operational improvements.

### Supplier Analysis

* Supplier B is the most efficient supplier.
* Supplier A contributes the highest logistics costs.

### Inventory Management

* Stock levels are significantly above reorder points.
* Inventory is evenly distributed across warehouses.

### Logistics Cost Analysis

* Transportation costs are evenly distributed among Air, Rail, and Road modes.
* Air transport delivers the best performance efficiency.
